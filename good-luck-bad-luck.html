Part 2: Prediction

Reading Strategy: Prediction

Good readers make predictions using:
  • the title
  • story clues
  • what they already know
Predictions can change while we read.
Clue 1

The title is Good Luck, Bad Luck.
Clue 2

Characters may disagree about whether events are lucky or unlucky.
Clue 3

One event may lead to another event.

Which prediction is BEST?

The story will only explain games of chance.
Events that seem bad at first may later become helpful, and vice versa.
The characters will never face problems.

Part 3: Reading Skill Prep — Cause & Effect

Reading Skill: Cause & Effect

A cause is why something happens.

An effect is what happens because of it.

Good readers ask:
  • What caused this event?
  • What happened next?
  • How did this change the story?

Practice

Cause: A horse runs away.

What is the best effect?

Nothing changes in the story.
The characters react differently and new events happen.
Everyone immediately becomes rich.

Now Read the Story

Part 4: After-Reading Quick Check

After Reading Strategy: Think About Perspective

Perspective means how different people think or feel about the same event.

Good readers ask:
  • Who thinks this is lucky?
  • Who thinks this is unlucky?
  • Does the situation change later?

Part 5: Cause → Effect Match

Choose one cause and one matching effect.

Cause

The horse runs away.
The horse returns with more horses.
A new problem happens later.

Effect

People think the event is good luck.
People first think something bad happened.
Readers realize situations can change again.

Part 6: Perspective Practice

Different characters may see the same event differently.

Which answer BEST shows perspective?

Some people think an event is unlucky, but another person stays calm because the situation may change later.

Part 7: Theme Practice

A theme is the lesson or message in a story.

Which theme BEST fits this story?

Every bad event stays bad forever.
People should not judge situations too quickly because events can change over time.
Horses solve every problem.
