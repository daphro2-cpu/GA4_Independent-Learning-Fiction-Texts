Part 2: Prediction

Reading Strategy: Prediction

Good readers make predictions before and during reading. A prediction is a smart guess based on the title, pictures, story clues, and what we already know.

This story is a Cinderella tale from China, so readers can use what they know about Cinderella stories to predict character problems, magical help, and a change in the main character’s life.

Look at the Clues

Clue 1

The title is Yeh-Shen: A Cinderella Tale from China.
Clue 2

Yeh-Shen is treated unfairly by her stepmother.
Clue 3

Kindness, magic, and courage may help Yeh-Shen.

Which prediction is BEST?

Yeh-Shen will become cruel because others are cruel to her.
Yeh-Shen may face unfair treatment, but magical help and her good character may lead to a better future.
The story will mostly explain how to catch fish for dinner.

Part 3: Reading Skill Prep — Character Traits

Reading Skill: Character Traits

Character traits describe what a character is like on the inside.

Readers infer character traits by looking at:
  • what the character says
  • what the character does
  • how the character treats others
  • how the character responds to problems
A strong answer uses a trait, a story clue, and an explanation.

Practice

Clue: Yeh-Shen is treated unfairly, but she is still gentle and kind.

Which character trait best fits Yeh-Shen?

selfish
kind and patient
careless and cruel

Now Read the Story

Read Yeh-Shen: A Cinderella Tale from China. While reading, look for:
  • how Yeh-Shen is treated
  • what Yeh-Shen is like as a character
  • how the stepmother and stepsister act
  • what magical help Yeh-Shen receives
  • how this story is similar to or different from other Cinderella stories
  • what lesson the story teaches

Part 4: After-Reading Quick Check

After Reading Strategy: Check Characters and Story Elements

After reading, good readers stop and check the important story elements:
  • main character
  • problem
  • helper or magical help
  • important object
  • ending
  • lesson/theme

Part 5: Compare & Contrast

Compare means to tell how things are similar. Contrast means to tell how things are different.

For this story, compare and contrast Yeh-Shen with the stepmother or compare this story with another Cinderella story.

Which answer shows a good comparison?

Yeh-Shen and her stepmother are exactly the same.
Yeh-Shen is kind and patient, but the stepmother is unfair and cruel.
Yeh-Shen and her stepmother are both magical fish.

Part 6: Character → Evidence Match

Choose one character idea and one matching evidence card.

Character Idea

Yeh-Shen is kind and gentle.
The stepmother is unfair.
Yeh-Shen needs help to change her situation.

Evidence

The stepmother treats Yeh-Shen badly and favours her own daughter.
Magical help gives Yeh-Shen a chance to go to the festival.
Yeh-Shen cares for the fish and does not act cruelly.

Part 7: Story Elements Practice

Cinderella tales often have similar story elements, but each version has special details.

Which story element fits Yeh-Shen?

Yeh-Shen has no problem and no unfair treatment.
Yeh-Shen is treated unfairly, receives magical help, and her life changes.
Yeh-Shen tricks everyone and steals from the king.

Part 8: Theme Practice

A theme is the lesson or message in a story. A strong theme is not just one event. It explains what the story teaches us.

Which theme BEST fits this story?

Being cruel to others always makes people happy.
Kindness and courage can help people face unfair situations.
Magic shoes are the only important thing in life.

Part 9: Worksheet Preparation

Your worksheet may ask you to compare characters, explain story elements, or support a character trait with evidence.

A strong answer should include:
  • a clear idea
  • a story clue
  • an explanation of what the clue shows

Choose the strongest worksheet answer.

Yeh-Shen is nice. The story has magic.
Yeh-Shen is kind and patient because she stays gentle even when her stepmother treats her unfairly. This shows she has a good heart.
The stepmother is kind because she helps Yeh-Shen go to the festival.
