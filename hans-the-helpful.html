Part 2: Prediction

Reading Strategy: Prediction

Good readers make predictions before and during reading. A prediction is a smart guess based on the title, pictures, story clues, and what we already know.

Prediction helps us focus on what may happen next and check whether our thinking changes as we read.

Look at the Clues

Clue 1

The title is Hans the Helpful.
Clue 2

Hans wants to help people and receives gifts.
Clue 3

Hans does not always carry or use the gifts in the best way.

Which prediction is BEST?

Hans will become a king because he is helpful.
Hans may try to help, but his choices with the gifts may cause problems.
Hans will never meet anyone in the story.

Part 3: Reading Skill Prep — Sequence + Action & Result

Reading Skill: Sequence

Sequence means the order that things happen in a story. We can use words like first, next, then, and finally.

Action & Result means noticing what a character does and what happens because of that action. Good readers ask:
  • What did Hans receive?
  • How did Hans carry or use it?
  • What happened next?
  • What does this show about Hans?

Practice

Action: Hans puts a small needle into a hay-cart.

What is the most likely result?

The needle becomes bigger.
Hans may not be able to find the needle later.
The hay-cart turns into butter.

Now Read the Story

Read Hans the Helpful. While reading, look for:
  • who Hans helps
  • what Hans receives each time
  • how Hans carries each gift
  • what happens to each gift
  • what Hans’s actions show about him

Part 4: After-Reading Quick Check

After Reading Strategy: Check and Think Again

After we read, good readers check the order of events and think about why each problem happened. For this story, ask: What did Hans do?, What happened next?, and what does this show about Hans?

Part 5: Sequence Practice

Choose the event that happened at each point in the story.

1. What happens first?

Hans helps people and begins receiving gifts.
Hans carries butter in his sleeve.
Hans carries his cousin on his shoulder.

2. What happens next?

Hans becomes rich.
Hans receives gifts but handles some of them in the wrong way.
Hans refuses to help anyone.

3. What happens again and again?

Hans solves every problem perfectly.
Hans carefully keeps every gift safe.
Hans tries to follow advice, but he uses it in the wrong situation.

Part 6: Action → Result Match

The worksheet may ask you to explain what happened to Hans and why. Matching actions with results helps you prepare strong answers.

Choose one action and one matching result.

Hans’s Actions

Hans puts the needle into the hay-cart.
Hans carries butter in his sleeve.
Hans carries a heavy person or animal in a difficult way.

Results

The butter melts and is ruined.
The trip becomes tiring and uncomfortable.
He cannot find the small needle later.

Part 7: Character Understanding

Hans wants to help, but he does not always think carefully. A good character answer should explain both:
  • what is good about Hans
  • what problem Hans has

Which sentence best describes Hans?

Hans is mean because he wants bad things to happen.
Hans is kind and helpful, but he often follows advice without thinking about the new situation.
Hans is careful because he always keeps every gift safe.

Part 8: Worksheet Preparation

Your worksheet may ask you to explain the order of events, what happened to Hans’s gifts, or what kind of person Hans is. A strong answer should include:
  • a clear idea about Hans
  • one story example
  • an explanation of what the example shows

Choose the strongest worksheet answer.

Hans is helpful because he helps. He gets many things.
Hans is kind because he helps many people, but he needs to think more carefully. For example, when he carries gifts in the wrong way, they are lost or ruined.
Hans is bad because all the gifts disappear.
