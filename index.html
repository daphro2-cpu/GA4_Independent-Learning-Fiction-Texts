Complete the pre-reading and post-reading activities before finishing your Short Reads Plus worksheet.

How to Use This Page

  • Before reading: learn key vocabulary and prepare one useful reading strategy.
  • Read the story: look carefully for clues, actions, problems, and results.
  • After reading: complete the quick check and worksheet skill practice.
  • Then: finish your paper worksheet using evidence from the text.
